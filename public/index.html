Kari’s Law and RAY BAUM’S Act

Federal requirements for covered phone systems address direct 9-1-1 dialing without an outside-line prefix, emergency-call notifications, and dispatchable location information. Work with your phone provider or system administrator to verify your system’s configuration and the requirements that apply to it.
